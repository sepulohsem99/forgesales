The problem

Selling through WhatsApp works — until it doesn't.

Every order is a manual conversation. That's fine at ten orders a week. At fifty, things start slipping through and you can't see where.

01

Enquiries go unanswered

A message at 11pm sits under twenty others by morning. That customer already bought from someone who replied faster.

02

You answer the same questions daily

Price? Got stock? Can post to Sabah? You've typed the same replies hundreds of times, and it's the same questions tomorrow.

03

Product management is guesswork

Prices live in three places. An old photo is still circulating. Someone orders something you stopped selling last month.

04

There's nothing to send people to

Running a Facebook ad? The only link you have is a phone number. Every single visitor needs a conversation before they can buy.

The solution

A store that answers for you, 24 hours a day.

Customers browse your full catalogue, see real prices and stock, and check out — without waiting for you to reply. You still talk to them, just not about whether something is in stock.

The difference isn't that you stop using WhatsApp. It's that WhatsApp becomes where you close the sale and answer real questions — not where you do inventory checks forty times a day.

What you get

Everything a real storefront needs. Nothing you'll never use.

No bloated plugin stack, no features nobody asked for. Each package builds on the one before it, so you only pay for what your business is ready to use.

How it works

Five steps, about two weeks.

You don't need to know anything technical. You send us your products and business details, we build the store, you approve it, we launch it.

Pricing

One-time price. No monthly surprise.

You pay once for the build. Domain, hosting and payment gateway fees are separate because they're registered in your name — you own them, not us.
